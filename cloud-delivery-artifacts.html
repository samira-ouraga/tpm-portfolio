About these artifacts

These are sanitized, retrospective examples created for this portfolio to illustrate TPM methods. They are not original employer documents. Confirmed outcomes are identified separately from illustrative planning and governance content.

01 / PROGRAM PLAN

Program plan

Purpose: Coordinated infrastructure provisioning for a partner engineering team, including network policies, events, storage, and access requirements. The work was delivered on time.

Illustrative workstreams

  • Capture partner requirements for cluster networking, events, storage and permissions
  • Confirm provisioning prerequisites and handoff expectations
  • Coordinate readiness for partner team validation

Boundaries / not represented

  • Undocumented service-level objectives
  • Production details, resource names, credentials or internal configurations

Planning checkpoints (illustrative): clarify scope → confirm prerequisites → complete technical work → validate outcome → hand off.
