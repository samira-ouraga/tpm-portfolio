About these artifacts

These are sanitized, retrospective examples created for this portfolio to illustrate TPM methods. They are not original employer documents. Confirmed outcomes are identified separately from illustrative planning and governance content.

01 / PROGRAM PLAN

Program plan

Purpose: Coordinated with engineering on a revised recycle/test approach, supported validation through a pilot, and helped update the procedure to reduce hardware rework time.

Illustrative workstreams

  • Identify repeat rework steps and the turnaround-time opportunity
  • Coordinate engineering review and pilot validation
  • Update the approved workflow and communicate the process change

Boundaries / not represented

  • Hardware-specific confidential instructions, test thresholds or part numbers
  • Unverified cost savings, defect rates or production-wide extrapolations

Planning checkpoints (illustrative): clarify scope → confirm prerequisites → complete technical work → validate outcome → hand off.
