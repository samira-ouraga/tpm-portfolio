01 · MICROSOFT · AZURECross-team infrastructure delivery

Coordinating Azure infrastructure and Kubernetes delivery

Helping a partner engineering team move forward by translating its infrastructure requirements into a coordinated technical delivery plan.

Challenge

A sister engineering team needed Kubernetes infrastructure before it could proceed with its own Azure work. Provisioning depended on clarifying networking policies, event and storage requirements, and permissions.

My ownership & TPM skills

Gathered partner requirements, clarified scope and dependencies, coordinated provisioning and permissions, agreed on delivery timing, and tracked blockers through handoff.

Execution

Organized the infrastructure work and aligned the technical prerequisites with the requesting team. Maintained a delivery plan that preserved time for the downstream team to continue its work.

Outcome

Delivered the Kubernetes cluster work on time. The partner team subsequently used the infrastructure in its Azure resource-query work.

TPM capabilities: requirements discovery · dependency mapping · cross-team coordination · schedule management · technical handoff

03 · AMAZON PROJECT LEO · AEROSPACEMeasured operational improvement

Reducing satellite hardware rework time by approximately 50%

A repeat hardware rework process created unnecessary turnaround time during production operations.

Challenge

Rebuilding hardware through the existing process took approximately two hours per rework cycle, creating avoidable production delay.

My ownership & TPM skills

Identified an improvement opportunity, coordinated with engineering, supported a pilot to validate a revised recycle/test approach, and helped update the standard operating procedure.

Execution

Worked with engineering to validate an alternative procedure before incorporating it into the documented production workflow.

Outcome

Reduced the rework cycle from approximately two hours to one hour — a roughly 50% reduction in rework time.

TPM capabilities: process improvement · engineering collaboration · risk-aware validation · change adoption · measurable outcomes
