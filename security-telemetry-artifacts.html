About these artifacts

These are sanitized, retrospective examples created for this portfolio to illustrate TPM methods. They are not original employer documents. Confirmed outcomes are identified separately from illustrative planning and governance content.

01 / PROGRAM PLAN

Program plan

Purpose: Owned worldwide Core Isolation telemetry work, created dashboards, defined success metrics, managed product backlogs, and shared findings and recommendations with leadership.

Illustrative workstreams

  • Define telemetry questions and useful security-feature KPIs
  • Maintain data visibility and reporting dashboards
  • Communicate analysis, trends and recommendations to stakeholders

Boundaries / not represented

  • Internal telemetry values, confidential device data and customer information
  • Claims of experiments or decisions that were not part of the verified work

Planning checkpoints (illustrative): clarify scope → confirm prerequisites → complete technical work → validate outcome → hand off.
